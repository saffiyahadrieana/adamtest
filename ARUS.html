Before a flood

  • Pack a go-bag: water, food, torch, medicine.
  • Put IDs in a waterproof bag.
  • Find your nearest evacuation centre.
  • Plan two routes out.

During a flood

  • Move to higher ground early.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Switch off power only if it is safe.
  • Follow official evacuation orders.
  • Do not attempt water rescues yourself. Call 999.

After a flood

  • Wait for official clearance to return.
  • Avoid wet electrical items.
  • Use safe drinking water.
  • Photograph damage from a safe spot.
  • See a doctor if exposed to dirty floodwater.

Flood Ready

1. What is a flash flood?

Heavy rain arrives faster than drains and rivers can carry it. In cities, roads and concrete send water to low ground within minutes. Warning signs: dark clouds, rising drains, muddy fast water.

2. Your go-bag

Water, ready food, medicine, torch, power bank, copies of IDs in a waterproof bag, and extra care items for children, elders and anyone with a disability.

3. Evacuation centres

Temporary centres open when authorities activate them. Bring IDs, medicine, bedding and snacks. Elders, babies and the disabled are helped first.

4. Giving help well

Ask what is needed before donating. Coordinate with local groups so one street is not flooded with rice while another has none.

5. Check before you share

Look for who posted it, when, and where. Share official sources first. If unsure, do not forward.
